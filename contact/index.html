Where we work in Clarington

From Bowmanville, Courtice, Newcastle Village, Historic Downtown Bowmanville, Orono, Port Darlington, Wilmot Creek, Hampton, Enniskillen, Tyrone, Maple Grove and Newtonville, we cover every Clarington neighbourhood — plus the surrounding Durham communities.

Heritage King Street storefronts in downtown Bowmanville. Historic Downtown Bowmanville holds close to two hundred businesses in brick buildings that mostly date to the 1800s, with the deep recessed entranceways and tall storefront openings of the period. Most of those entrances are aluminum doors that were retrofitted into masonry openings which have been settling for well over a century, so the frame was never truly square to start with and every winter nudges it a little further. Squaring the door to the opening it actually has — rather than to the catalogue dimension — is what makes a downtown Bowmanville door latch again and stay latched.

Courtice growth along Highway 2 and the new GO corridor. Courtice is one of the fastest-growing communities in Durham Region, and the plazas and commercial units going up along Highway 2, Courtice Road and around the future GO station bring a wave of brand-new aluminum entrances with them. New does not mean trouble-free: builder-grade closers get set at the factory and never adjusted for an Ontario winter, and a plaza door cycling hundreds of times a day chews through a bottom pivot in its first couple of seasons. We service these openings as wear items rather than waiting for the tenant to call on a Saturday night because the unit will not lock.
