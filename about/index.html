About Soper Creek Door & Hardware

Soper Creek Door and Hardware started the way most door companies do: with the service calls nobody else wanted. A shop on King Street West that could not lock up at closing. A Courtice plaza unit whose brand-new entrance had never latched properly since the first cold snap. A steel shop door on a concession road north of Bowmanville that had been shouldered shut for two winters because the frame had been pushed out of square and nobody wanted to look at it.

We are a Clarington door repair outfit, not a general contractor with a van and a drill. We work on aluminum storefronts, hollow metal doors and frames, hinges, pivots, closers, thresholds, locks and exit hardware — commercial, industrial and residential — across Bowmanville, Courtice, Newcastle Village, Orono and the rural hamlets, plus the wider Durham Region and Greater Toronto Area. We fix what actually caused the problem, which on a Clarington door is usually the frame, the pivot or the season rather than the lock everyone blames.

We are fully insured and WSIB covered, we guarantee our workmanship in writing, and we quote a fixed scope after looking at the opening. No estimate over the phone that changes once we arrive, and no upsell to a new door when the one you have has years left in it.
