FAQs

Hollow Metal Door & Frame Repair — your questions

Do you actually come out to farm shops and outbuildings north of the 401?

Yes, regularly. Orono, Newtonville, Hampton, Enniskillen, Tyrone and Maple Grove are ordinary calls. We bring hinges, closers, strikes and seals on the truck so a rural visit does not become two, because the second trip is what makes country work expensive everywhere else.

Our back door was pried during a break-in. Repairable, or is it scrap?

Usually repairable. A pry attack deforms the lock edge and tears the strike out of the jamb, and both of those are replaceable: the jamb is drawn back and re-anchored, new strike material goes in, and the leaf edge is dressed straight. If the skin has split from the core along the lock edge, the leaf gets replaced. Either way the opening is made secure on the first visit.

Wind keeps catching the shop door and throwing it back. What stops that?

An overhead stop that is genuinely rated for it, plus a closer with the backcheck set. On an open lot the door catches the full wind with nothing to break it, and what gives way first is normally the top hinge or the closer arm rather than the door. We fit the stop, size the closer for that exposure, and where hinge screws have already torn we move into solid backing or a continuous hinge.
