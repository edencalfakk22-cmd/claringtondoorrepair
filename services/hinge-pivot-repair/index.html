FAQs

Hinge & Pivot Replacement — your questions

The hinge screws keep pulling out of our old door frame — can that be fixed?

Yes, and it is a common call in older Bowmanville, Orono and Newcastle homes. We repair the stripped pocket properly with longer fasteners into solid framing or a repaired jamb, rather than packing the hole with filler. Done right the hinge holds and the door stops sagging.

Is a continuous hinge worth it on a busy commercial door?

On a high-cycle entrance, usually yes. A continuous hinge carries the door along its full height instead of at three points, so it resists the sag that keeps pulling a busy shop or plaza door out of alignment. We will tell you honestly when a standard hinge set is fine and the money is better spent elsewhere.

Our storefront door is dropping at the lock corner again — did the last repair fail?

Not necessarily. If the pivot was replaced but the frame is out of square or the closer is over-tightened, the new hardware is being loaded wrong and will wear the same way. We check the pivot, the frame square and the closer setting together, which is why our repairs tend to stay put.
