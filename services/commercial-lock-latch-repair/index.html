Commercial Locks, Deadlatches & Exit Devices — your questions

The shop will not lock at closing. Where does that usually start?

With the door, not the lock. If the leaf has dropped or the frame has moved, the bolt no longer meets the strike opening squarely and cannot throw its full length. Staff then force the key, which is how cylinders wear out and keys snap. We find where the bolt is landing, put the door back where it belongs, and adjust the strike afterwards.
