FAQs

Commercial Locks, Deadlatches & Exit Devices — your questions

Our shop will not lock at closing time — what usually causes that?

Most often the door has dropped on a worn pivot or the frame has shifted, so the deadlatch is missing the strike. Cold weather makes it worse because everything contracts and the closer slows. We re-align the door, correct the strike, and repair or replace the lock so the premises secure properly again.

Can you service panic bars and exit devices on Clarington commercial buildings?

Yes — repair, adjustment and replacement of panic and exit hardware on commercial and industrial doors. A door on an exit route has to open from the inside in one motion, so a sticking or seized device is a life-safety issue rather than routine maintenance, and we prioritise it accordingly.

Do you install electric strikes and buzzer entry on commercial doors?

Yes, on aluminum, hollow metal and glass entrance doors, including correcting the door and frame so the strike lines up reliably. We coordinate with your access-control or alarm provider where one is already in place rather than duplicating what you have.
